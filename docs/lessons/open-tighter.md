# Open tighter into tables that fight back

From early and middle position, more players are left to act. Against loose or aggressive
players behind, marginal opens lose: they get called or 3-bet too often.

## The idea

An open from the HJ has to get through four players. Against Calling stations, opens with
medium high cards still win a little, mostly with the bigger size that charges their calls.
Against Maniacs who 3-bet and call wide, the same hands are clear folds.

## Rule of thumb

- Against a loose-passive table, open your playable hands bigger (4 bb) and fold the rest.
- Against an aggressive table, cut marginal opens such as K9o, A8o, QTo and small pairs
  from early position.
- Don't open-limp to "see a cheap flop". The engine never prefers it here.

## The engine's numbers

HJ, unopened, no ante (EV in bb, against folding):

| Hand | Calling-station table | Maniac table |
|---|---|---|
| K9o | open to 4 +0.18 (close) | fold; open −0.51 (clear) |
| A8o | open to 4 +0.38 (close) | fold; open −0.26 (close) |
| QTo | open to 4 +0.07 (close) | fold; open −0.53 (clear) |
| 55 | open to 4 +0.22 (close) | fold; open −0.52 (clear) |

## Limits

Facing a re-raise, the engine stacks off or folds, so 3-bet pots are cruder than real play.
